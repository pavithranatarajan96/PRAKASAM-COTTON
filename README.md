PRAKASAM COTTON
Overview
This project focuses on analysing inventory, sales, Amazon inventory, returned products, and Purchase Order (PO) quantities using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.
The main objective is to compare inventory availability with sales performance and understand the relationship between stock levels, product returns, and purchase orders.
Tools Used
•	Microsoft Excel
•	Microsoft Power BI
•	Pivot Tables
•	VLOOKUP
•	Data Cleaning and Transformation
•	Data Visualization
Data Cleaning and Transformation
•	Cleaned the available data to prepare it for analysis.
•	Used VLOOKUP and Paste Special to transfer the required data into specific sheets.
•	Consolidated 5 sheets into 2 sheets containing the required data.
•	Created Pivot Tables to summarize inventory, sales, and PO quantities.
•	Prepared the summarized data for Power BI visualization.
Project Analysis
1. Inventory Analysis
•	Analysed company inventory and Amazon inventory.
•	Compared total inventory with sales quantities.
•	Identified the difference between available inventory and sales movement.
•	Analysed sellable and unsellable inventory.
2. Sales Analysis
•	Compared sales quantities with available inventory.
•	Evaluated product sales movement.
•	Identified product categories with higher inventory and comparatively lower sales.
3. Amazon Returns Analysis
•	Analysed products returned from Amazon.
•	Included returned products in the overall inventory analysis.
•	Reviewed the contribution of returned products to available stock.
4. Purchase Order Analysis
•	Analysed confirmed and reversed PO quantities.
•	Compared the original order quantity with reversed quantities.
•	Calculated the final confirmed PO quantity.
•	Reviewed the relationship between purchase orders and inventory planning.
Types of Analysis
•	Descriptive Analysis: Summarizes inventory, sales, returned products, and PO quantities.
•	Diagnostic Analysis: Examines the differences between inventory and sales and reviews possible reasons for stock variations.
•	Predictive Analysis: Provides an understanding of possible future inventory and sales trends based on current data.
•	Prescriptive Analysis: Suggests improvements in inventory monitoring, stock planning, and purchase order management.
Power BI Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard provides visual insights into:
•	Inventory availability
•	Sales quantity
•	Amazon inventory
•	Returned products
•	Sellable and unsellable stock
•	Confirmed and reversed PO quantities
Key Findings
•	Inventory quantity is comparatively higher than sales quantity in certain product categories.
•	Amazon inventory and returned products contribute to the overall stock position.
•	Comparing sellable and unsellable inventory helps understand actual stock availability.
•	PO analysis provides visibility into confirmed and reversed order quantities.
•	Inventory and sales comparison supports better stock planning and monitoring.
Business Recommendations
•	Monitor inventory levels regularly.
•	Improve stock planning based on sales movement.
•	Track Amazon returned products separately.
•	Review sellable and unsellable inventory.
•	Monitor confirmed and reversed purchase orders.
•	Avoid unnecessary additional purchases when sufficient stock is available.
Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel and Power BI can be used to analyse inventory, sales, returns, and purchase order data.
The analysis helps understand stock availability, sales performance, and purchase order status. The Power BI visualizations provide a clear overview of the data and support better inventory management and business decision-making.
